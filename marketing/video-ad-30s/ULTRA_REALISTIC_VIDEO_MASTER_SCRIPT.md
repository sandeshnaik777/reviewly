## 🏆 The "Anti-AI Look" Visual Consistency Rules

To prevent the video from looking like generic, plastic, cartoonish "AI-generated slop", follow these 4 mandatory cinematography rules across all prompts:

1. **Consistent Real Human Character Profile ("Rohan - The Store Owner"):**
   - *Subject:* A 34-year-old handsome Indian business owner, short neatly trimmed dark hair, well-groomed light stubble, warm relatable eyes.
   - *Wardrobe:* Deep navy-blue linen button-up shirt with sleeves rolled up, plain white crew-neck undershirt, heavy olive-green canvas storekeeper apron with brass buckle.
   - *Consistency Rule:* Every prompt referencing the owner uses this exact physical DNA so his face and clothes remain 100% identical between shots.

2. **Consistent Customer Profile ("Ananya - The Customer"):**
   - *Subject:* A 26-year-old stylish Indian woman, dark brown hair in a loose low ponytail, natural everyday makeup, gentle smile.
   - *Wardrobe:* Plain beige textured cotton top, delicate thin silver wristwatch, holding a real midnight black smartphone.

3. **Consistent Physical Environment & Real Lighting:**
   - *Location:* Modern premium artisanal cafe / boutique store in Bengaluru, India.
   - *Surfaces:* Reclaimed dark oak wood counter, matte brass accents, warm white subway tile wall in soft focus background, real sunlight streaming through clean street-side glass windows.
   - *Lighting:* Diffused 5600K afternoon window daylight with warm 3200K tungsten interior practical lamps. Natural specular falloff — **NO oversaturated neon, NO CGI glow**.

4. **Cinema Camera & Lens Specs (Emulating High-End Commercial Film):**
   - `Shot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF, Cooke S4/i 35mm and 50mm T2.0 prime cinema lenses, natural 35mm Kodak Vision3 film grain, organic skin pores and micro-textures, cinematic shallow depth of field, real optical motion blur --style raw`.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Production Guide (How to Assemble in CapCut / Premiere)

1. **Step 1 — Create your Video Timeline (30.00s at 60 FPS):**
   - Create a 1920x1080 (16:9) or 1080x1920 (9:16) sequence in CapCut / Premiere.
2. **Step 2 — Import your ElevenLabs Audio Track:**
   - Drop `ElevenLabs_...Viraj.mp3` onto Audio Track 1.
   - For English (27.09s), it leaves exactly 2.91s for the outro bumper.
   - For Hindi (28.60s), it leaves exactly 1.40s for the outro bumper.
   - For Kannada (28.92s), it leaves exactly 1.08s for the outro bumper.
   - For Tamil (29.15s), it leaves exactly 0.85s for the outro bumper.
3. **Step 3 — Generate Shots 1, 2, 3A, 4, 5:**
   - In Google Veo / Runway Gen-3 Alpha, paste the exact prompts above.
   - Set motion to `3` (natural, steady, non-warping).
4. **Step 4 — Insert Real UI Screen Recording for Shot 3B:**
   - Record `https://kindlyrate.com/r/demo-billing-counter` on your phone and cut into Shot 3B (0:13.5 to 0:18.0). This anchors the entire ad in reality and eliminates any "AI fake" impression.
5. **Step 5 — Add Subtitles & Sound Effects:**
   - Add NFC ping sound at `0:12.5`.
   - Add WhatsApp ping at `0:20.5`.
   - Add confetti swell at `0:26.0`.
   - Use **Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold 800** for all text overlays.
6. **Step 6 — Export:**
   - 4K UHD, 60fps, 35 Mbps bitrate.
